Who we are

Engineering-led. Commercially aware. Execution focused.

Majlis Energy is an independent project development and technical advisory company focused on energy and circular infrastructure.

Our foundation

Built on independent technical understanding.

Founded in 2018, Majlis Energy connects engineering feasibility with commercial viability.

Our approach has always been structured. We began with technology research and university collaboration, moved through feasibility analysis and technology evaluation, and evolved into a business providing technical advisory, project development and commercial coordination for industrial infrastructure projects.

We work with industrial companies, project developers, engineering firms, technology providers, investors, banks and strategic partners.

Our position

Clear about what we do — and what we do not.

  • 01

  • 02

  • 03
