Our services

Independent expertise. At every project stage.

Independent technical advisory and project development support for industrial infrastructure, circular-economy and energy-transition projects.

Service areas

Four integrated capabilities.

Technical advisory and project development lead our work, supported by commercial preparation and the partnerships each project needs.

01

Technical Advisory

Independent, technology-neutral assessment of the technical basis of your project.

  • Technical due diligence for owners, investors and lenders
  • Technology evaluation, TRL and scale-up assessment
  • CAPEX and OPEX validation
  • Process engineering support and owner’s engineering
  • Engineering coordination, FEED review and EPC interface support
  • Independent technical review and bankability assessment
  • Plant performance, troubleshooting and operational optimisation
02

Project Development

Structure and coordinate industrial projects from early assessment through implementation support.

  • Project origination and development
  • Engineering coordination
  • Project planning and scheduling
  • Stakeholder management
  • Implementation support
03

Commercial & Project Finance Preparation

Connect engineering decisions with project economics and financing readiness.

  • Commercial and financial modelling
  • Project economics and sensitivity analysis
  • Financing-readiness and investor / lender information coordination
  • Commercial coordination across feedstock, offtake and partners

Majlis Energy does not provide regulated investment advice or generic financial services.

04

Strategic Partnerships

An enabling capability that brings the right expertise into each stage of a project.

  • Engineering partners
  • Technology providers
  • Commercial and industrial partners
  • Financial and investor partners
